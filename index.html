Commercial

LED lighting

We developed interactive LED lighting for studio F-Minus' 30 foot holiday sculpture: Frost. A large illuminated snowflake occupies the atrium of Brookfield Place in Toronto. Touching the surface causes the colors to shift

Regenerative Braking Demo

To profile KIA's regenerative braking technology, we developed an interactive demo. Users press car pedals to drive and brake a full size car wheel mounted in a kiosk. The brake also recharges a virtual battery displayed on the dashboard.

Illuminated Brain

Interactive model of a brain that responds to touch, displaying various patterns. Produced in collaboration with Globacore Interactive for Dassault Systèmes's Digital Twin exhibit at CES.

About

Devicist is the studio of Nick Stedman, an artist, creative technologist and university lecturer with over 20 years of experience building embodied experiences with electronics, coding, and fabrication. His award-winning robotic artworks have been showcased globally, including at Ars Electronica, the Ontario Science Center, SIGGRAPH and on Japanese TV. Since founding Devicist in 2014, Nick and his team have partnered with fabricators, agencies, architects, and creators on diverse projects, from permanent installations to trade show centerpieces.
